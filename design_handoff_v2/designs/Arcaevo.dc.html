SCIENCE & TRUST

The logic is deterministic. The AI only narrates.

Rules written from the literature decide every clinical call — never a language model. The AI rewrites them into plain English and answers your questions, but can’t invent a threshold. That’s the safety posture, and the trust story.

Accredited Irish labs

Dublin-fulfilled, CE-marked kits, processed in ISO-accredited EU laboratories.

Clinician-reviewed

A registered doctor signs off every result. Critical values are flagged to them, not coached by AI.

EU data residency

GDPR Article 9 explicit consent, encrypted in eu-west-1, never sold. Export or delete in one tap.

Wellness, not diagnosis

Optimisation for healthy people. We tell you, plainly, when to see your GP.
