01

Four decisions that shape everything

AUTH · EMAIL FIRST

No hidden addresses

Email + password, or a one-tap magic link — no social sign-in at launch. Apple's Sign-in-with-Apple mandate (App Review 4.8) only applies when an app offers a third-party login like Google; email-only auth is exempt. So no early user ever hides behind a relay address, and support can always reach a real inbox.

APPLE HEALTH ≠ APPLE LOGIN

Sync is a permission, not an account

Reading the Watch needs a HealthKit permission granted on the device — it has nothing to do with how the user signed in. An email-only account syncs Apple Health perfectly. Apple auth can be added later without touching the sync layer.

PURCHASE · WEB ONLY

The app never sells

All payment happens on arcaevo.com via Stripe. The iOS app is a free companion that links out to web checkout — permitted for EU storefronts — so no 15–30% App Store commission is baked into test pricing, and one billing system serves web and app.

ELIGIBILITY · GATE THE KIT

Gate the kit, not the account

Anyone, anywhere, can create a free account and buy Fusion — nothing ships. The Eircode check runs only at checkout for Essential and Performance, because those need a courier or a nurse. Outside Dublin, checkout becomes an early-access list, not a dead end.

The consent gate

Health data is special-category data under GDPR Article 9 — it needs explicit, separate, revocable consent before we process a single heartbeat. This screen appears once, right after email verification, on whichever surface the user touched first. It is never bundled into the terms checkbox.

Granular, not a wall of text

Three named purposes, each in one sentence. Required items are pre-explained, the optional one is off by default — the pattern the DPC expects.

Versioned & auditable

Each consent is stored with timestamp, wording version and surface. If the notice materially changes, users re-consent on next sign-in — a ready-made re-consent screen, not an email nobody reads.

Withdrawal is a first-class flow

Account → Data & privacy shows these same three switches. Turning off the required one triggers the account-closure flow (10) — honestly, with a full export offered first.

11

What's still missing — the audit

Everything a fully-fledged product needs that no page yet covered. Ranked: build-before-launch, build-soon, and later. The must-builds are now designed below — 12 Emails, 13 Bloodwork upload, 14 When things go wrong.

BEFORE LAUNCH — THE PRODUCT DOESN'T WORK WITHOUT THESE
Transactional email suite

Nine templates in the brand voice: verify, magic link, password reset, receipt, kit shipped, sample received, results ready (never results in the email), renewal reminder at 30 days, failed-payment notice.

Critical-value escalation

When a result needs a doctor today: clinician phones first, app shows a calm "your clinician needs a word" state — never a red number as a push notification. Needs an SOP, a script and an unreachable-after-3-attempts letter.

Renewal & dunning

Card expiring / charge failed: 3 retries over 14 days, grace period with full access, then a read-only "your data is safe, membership paused" state — never deletion by lapse.

Failed-sample recovery

Haemolysed, insufficient, or lost in post: proactive notice within 24h of the lab flag, replacement kit auto-dispatched free, and it doesn't count against the plan's test allowance.

Bloodwork upload (Fusion's engine)

Fusion is sold on "upload any past bloodwork" but the flow doesn't exist: PDF/photo upload → AI extraction → user confirms values & units → merged into the timeline flagged "self-reported".

Support hand-off

Help exists as a page; the flows need it in context — "Something wrong?" links on the sample journey, checkout and dunning states that open a pre-filled message, not a generic contact form.

SOON AFTER — TRUST & GROWTH
Waitlist nurture

Early-access joiners get a monthly "where we're opening next" note, live position in Account, and a founding-member window when their county opens. A silent waitlist is a churned waitlist.

GP share flow

"Share with your GP" as a first-class action: a clean clinician PDF with a revocable link — the single feature most likely to earn word-of-mouth from Irish doctors.

Referrals & gifting

Give a month / get a month, and a giftable Essential year with a printed card — health memberships are bought as gifts in December, reliably.

Passkeys & 2FA

Passkeys as the natural upgrade from magic links (same email-first model, no passwords to steal); optional TOTP for the security-conscious. Slots into Sign-in & security.

Sign in with Apple

Add later as a convenience, with relay addresses accepted by then — once the early-user cohort (real emails, reachable) is established. The auth model doesn't change.

Admin: eligibility & waitlist ops

The Admin console needs three new views: routing-key config, waitlist demand by county, and a consent-audit log. Expansion decisions should come from that dashboard.

LATER — WORTH A PLACEHOLDER, NOT A SPRINT
Household plans

Two adults, one bill, strictly separate health data. Couples ask for this within months.

Corporate / employer

Codes bought by a company, redeemed privately — employer never sees results, only uptake.

Android & other wearables

WHOOP, Oura and Garmin connect via APIs (no HealthKit) — the "soon" chips on the site become an OAuth connection screen in Settings.
